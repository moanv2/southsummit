04

Why AWS wins the GDPR conversation

Two facts answer the DPO's biggest objections before they're raised.
🇪🇸 Data stays in Spain
The AWS Europe (Spain) region (eu-south-2, Aragón) keeps patient data on Spanish soil. For an AEPD-facing DPO, "data never leaves the country" is a tender-winner and an objection-ender.
📋 Inherited compliance
AWS is a GDPR data processor with a signed DPA; Comprehend Medical & Textract are HIPAA-eligible. RedShield inherits a mountain of compliance — and cites it directly in the DPIA.
🔒 Tamper-proof evidence
S3 Object Lock (WORM) means the signed audit report cannot be altered after signing. CloudTrail records every access — bulletproof chain of custody if the AEPD ever asks.
🧠 No data egress to the model
Running Claude on Bedrock keeps the LLM inside AWS/EU. Kills the "you're shipping PHI to a US API endpoint" objection cold.
