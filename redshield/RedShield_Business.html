05

Customer pain points

The buyer: the DPO of a ~600-bed private hospital. Legally mandated to exist (LOPDGDD Art. 34).
Career risk
Fired for a patient-data leak they had no mechanism to detect or prevent. "You will never have to tell the board, or the AEPD, that you had no mechanism."
Regulatory exposure
Mandatory DPIA + GDPR Art. 32 obligations today. AEPD issued €40M in fines in 2025; ceiling is 4% of global turnover. Marina Salud fined €500k (Apr 2025).
Documented-but-untested gap
DPIAs exist on paper but nobody adversarially tested the agent. #1 healthcare fine cause in 2025 = inadequate technical measures.
Over-broad access
Named AEPD failure: "software letting staff access more patient data than necessary" — maps exactly to the tool/retrieval chokepoint.
False contractual comfort
Signed a BAA/DPA with the AI vendor and assume liability transferred — but nobody tested whether the controls actually hold.
No in-house capability
No ML red-team team; can't run thousands of attack prompts. Need an outsider who speaks DPIA, not engineer-eval.
⚠️ Honesty flag — read before pitching
This is warm intent, not payment — no one has paid or begged yet. The funnel is the hypothesis; the conversion rates are targets, not results. Strongest framing: "the gap is real and the buyer is legally mandated to exist — what's unvalidated is whether they'll pay an outsider to attack their live agent. That's exactly what this week's experiment tests."
